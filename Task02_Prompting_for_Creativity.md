==> Task 02: Prompting for Creativity
-------------------------------------------
Internship Program – Skillcross Technologies

~ Task Objective - 
			To explore how prompt structure, tone, and examples influence creative outputs generated by AI models.

~ Chosen Creative Task - 
			Short Story Generation


~ Prompt Variant 1: Simple Prompt
	Prompt:
	----------------------------------
		Write a short story about friendship.
	Output Summary:
	----------------------------------
		The story was basic and general. It had a simple plot with minimal emotional depth or creative detail.


~ Prompt Variant 2: Detailed Prompt
	Prompt :
	----------------------------------
		Write a short story about friendship between two college students, focusing on trust and support during difficult times.
	Output Summary : 
	----------------------------------
		The story had better structure, relatable characters, and emotional depth compared to the simple prompt.


~ Prompt Variant 3: Creative Prompt with Tone & Persona (Few-shot Style)
	Prompt:
	----------------------------------
		You are a creative storyteller. Write an emotional short story about friendship between two college students who overcome a misunderstanding.
		Use expressive language and a hopeful tone. Limit the story to 150 words.
	Output Summary : 
	-----------------------------------	
		The story was emotionally rich, creative, and well-structured. The tone was hopeful, and the characters felt realistic and engaging.


 Comparison & Analysis
----------------------------------
  Prompt 1 produced a generic output due to lack of guidance.
	Prompt 2 improved creativity by adding context and focus.
	Prompt 3 generated the most creative and detailed output because it defined tone, role, constraints, and style.

~ Conclusion
----------------------------------
  This task demonstrated that creative AI outputs depend heavily on prompt design. Adding role instructions, tone, and 
	constraints significantly enhances creativity and relevance.
